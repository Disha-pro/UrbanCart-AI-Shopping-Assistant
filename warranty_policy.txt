URBANCART — WARRANTY POLICY
Version: Q2 2024 | Approved by: Customer Experience

1. WARRANTY COVERAGE
1.1 Electronics carry a manufacturer warranty ranging from 6 months to 24 months.
1.2 The warranty period starts from the date of delivery.
1.3 Warranty covers manufacturing defects only, not physical or liquid damage.

2. WARRANTY CLAIMS
2.1 To claim warranty, the customer must provide the order ID and a description of the defect.
2.2 Claims are routed to the manufacturer's authorized service center.
2.3 Turnaround for warranty repair/replacement is typically 10-15 business days.

3. EXCLUSIONS
3.1 Damage due to misuse, unauthorized repair, or normal wear and tear is not covered.
3.2 Consumable parts (batteries beyond 6 months, cables) may have limited coverage.
