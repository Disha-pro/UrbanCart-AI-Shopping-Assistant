URBANCART — PAYMENT & CASH ON DELIVERY POLICY
Version: Q2 2024 | Approved by: Payments & Risk

1. ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS
1.1 UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, UrbanCart Wallet, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
1.2 EMI is available on orders above Rs 3000 with select banks.

2. CASH ON DELIVERY (COD)
2.1 COD is available on orders up to Rs 20000.
2.2 COD may not be available for certain pincodes or high-value items.
2.3 A nominal COD handling fee of Rs 20 may apply on some orders.

3. PAYMENT SECURITY
3.1 UrbanCart never stores full card details; payments are processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateways.
3.2 UrbanCart will never ask for your OTP, PIN, or full card number over chat or call.

4. FAILED / DUPLICATE PAYMENTS
4.1 If money is debited but the order is not confirmed, it is auto-refunded within 5-7 business days.
