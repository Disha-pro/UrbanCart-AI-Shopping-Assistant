URBANCART — SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY
Version: Q2 2024 | Approved by: Logistics & Operations

1. DELIVERY TIMELINES
1.1 Metro cities: 2-4 business days.
1.2 Non-metro cities: 4-7 business days.
1.3 Remote / rural pincodes: 7-10 business days.

2. SHIPPING CHARGES
2.1 Orders above Rs 499: free standard shipping.
2.2 Orders below Rs 499: a flat shipping fee of Rs 40 applies.
2.3 Express delivery (where available) costs an extra Rs 99.

3. SERVICEABILITY
3.1 Delivery availability depends on the pincode entered at checkout.
3.2 Some remote pincodes may not be serviceable for Cash on Delivery.

4. ORDER TRACKING
4.1 A tracking link is shared via SMS and app notification once the order is shipped.
4.2 Orders not delivered within 15 days of purchase are eligible for a full refund.
