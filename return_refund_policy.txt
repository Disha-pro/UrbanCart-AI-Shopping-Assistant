URBANCART — RETURN & REFUND POLICY
Version: Q2 2024 | Approved by: Customer Experience & Legal

1. RETURN ELIGIBILITY
1.1 Apparel & Footwear: Returnable within 14 days of delivery, tags intact, unworn.
1.2 Electronics: Returnable within 7 days of delivery, original packaging and accessories required.
1.3 Home & Kitchen: Returnable within 10 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
1.4 Beauty & Personal Care: Non-returnable once the seal is opened, for hygiene reasons.

2. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
2.1 Innerwear, cosmetics with broken seals, and customized products cannot be returned.
2.2 Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page are not eligible for return or exchange.

3. REFUND PROCESS
3.1 Refunds are initiated within 24 hours of the returned item passing quality check.
3.2 Prepaid orders: refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
3.3 Cash on Delivery orders: refunded to the customer's UrbanCart Wallet or bank account within 7-10 business days.
3.4 Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the item was defective or wrong.

4. EXCHANGE
4.1 Size exchanges for Apparel & Footwear are free within 14 days, subject to stock availability.
4.2 If the requested size is unavailable, a full refund is issued instead.
